## Selected Research Reading

My current research interests include **Computer Vision, Visual Speech Recognition (VSR), Transformers, multimodal learning, and low-resource speech technologies**. Below are some of the papers I have studied as part of my research and independent learning.

### Foundations & Sequence Modeling

- [**Attention Is All You Need**](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762) — Vaswani et al.
- [**Learning Phrase Representations using RNN Encoder–Decoder for Statistical Machine Translation**](https://arxiv.org/abs/1406.1078) — Cho et al.

### Visual Speech Recognition

- [**Lip Reading Sentences in the Wild**](https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.05358) — Chung et al.
- [**Arabic Lip Reading With Limited Data Using Deep Learning**](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Arabic+Lip+Reading+With+Limited+Data+Using+Deep+Learning) — Jaber et al.
- [**An Arabic Visual Dataset for Visual Speech Recognition**](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=An+Arabic+Visual+Dataset+for+Visual+Speech+Recognition) — Elrefaei et al.
- [**Cross-Attention Fusion of Visual and Geometric Features for Large Vocabulary Arabic Lipreading**](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Cross-Attention+Fusion+of+Visual+and+Geometric+Features+for+Large+Vocabulary+Arabic+Lipreading) — Daou et al.
- [**Read My Lips: Artificial Intelligence Word-Level Arabic Lipreading System**](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Read+My+Lips+Artificial+Intelligence+Word-Level+Arabic+Lipreading+System) — Dweik et al.
- [**LRW-Persian: Lip-reading in the Wild Dataset for Persian Language**](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=LRW-Persian+Lip-reading+in+the+Wild+Dataset+for+Persian+Language) — Taghizadeh et al.

### Audio-Visual & Speech Recognition

- [**Robust Speech Recognition via Large-Scale Weak Supervision**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.04356) — Radford et al.
- [**MuAViC: A Multilingual Audio-Visual Corpus for Robust Speech Recognition and Robust Speech-to-Text Translation**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.00628) — Anwar et al.
- [**Overcoming Data Scarcity in Multi-Dialectal Arabic ASR via Whisper Fine-Tuning**](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Overcoming+Data+Scarcity+in+Multi-Dialectal+Arabic+ASR+via+Whisper+Fine-Tuning) — Özyılmaz et al.
