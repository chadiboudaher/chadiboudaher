I’m a master’s student in Computer and Communications Engineering at Lebanese International University. I work at the intersection of machine learning research and the engineering needed to make it useful.
My thesis focuses on visual speech recognition for Lebanese Arabic: a low-resource setting where collecting and carefully reviewing audiovisual data is as important as training a model. I also build with PyTorch, computer vision, and FastAPI.
What I’m working on
- Lebanese Arabic visual speech recognition — studying lip reading and audiovisual speech, including the challenges of dialect and code-switching.
- An audiovisual corpus builder — developing a research workflow for video processing, active speaker evidence, human review, and versioned dataset exports.
- Hands-on ML experiments — implementing models, comparing results, and learning from papers rather than treating a benchmark score as the whole story.
The sequence-modeling idea I keep coming back to
\[
\delta_t =
\frac{\partial \ell_t}{\partial h_t}
+
\left(\frac{\partial h_{t+1}}{\partial h_t}\right)^{\!\top}
\delta_{t+1}
\qquad \text{(BPTT)}
\]
Backpropagation through time traces how a model's error flows across a sequence. I like understanding the mechanism, implementing it, and seeing where it succeeds or fails.
